# Restaurant Sales Dashboard – Key Insights

## Overall Performance

• Total Revenue: ₹1,59,217.90
• Total Items Sold: 12,097

## Category Performance

• Italian category generated the highest revenue.
• American category generated the lowest revenue.
• Asian category had the highest number of items sold.

## Item Performance

• Korean Beef Bowl was the top revenue-generating item.

## Time-Based Analysis

• Revenue peaked around 12 PM, showing strong sales during lunchtime.
• February had the lowest monthly revenue.
• Revenue increased again in March after the February decline.

## Business Takeaways

• Italian items performed strongly in terms of revenue.
• Korean Beef Bowl was an important contributor to sales.
• Lunchtime appears to be the strongest sales period.
• The February decline can be investigated further to understand the reason behind lower sales.
• Category-wise analysis can help identify strong and weak-performing menu categories.

## Revenue Calculation

Revenue was calculated using the Price field because each order-detail row represents an item sold.

Revenue = SUM(Price)
